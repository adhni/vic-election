How this comparison works

Groups use the selected election’s Democratic nominee share of the two selected sides. They are descriptive voting bands, not a clustering model or a prediction. In 2025, Cuomo and Sliwa are combined only for this comparison: Cuomo’s Fight and Deliver votes are not Republican ballot-line votes. Other candidates and write-ins are excluded from the two-side percentage and shown in the details.

Two groups split at 50%; three use 45% and 55%; four use 40%, 50% and 60%; five use 40%, 45%, 55% and 60%; seven add 30% and 70%. Exact 50% ties have a neutral dot and an explicit tie label. Areas with no votes for either side appear separately in grey.

One dot means one mapped reporting area. Combined precincts have one dot because separate vote counts are unavailable. Precinct boundaries differ between elections, so dots are not matched across years. This is a count of areas, not voters or population. Source: NYC Board of Elections returns already loaded in this explorer.
