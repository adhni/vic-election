How counting rounds, sources and colours work

This is one ranked-choice election, not 14 separate elections or 14 preference rankings. Candidates are eliminated using the citywide count. Their ballots move to the next valid continuing choice, or become exhausted. We replay the same citywide elimination order within each precinct; we do not run separate precinct elections. Lurie’s final citywide share uses continuing votes, not all ballots cast.

The ballot-pattern archive and transcription of the official round table are preserved from DataDiver at a pinned revision. Our independent replay matches every candidate total, exhausted count, overvote count and blank count in all 14 rounds. The original official result archive was inaccessible during import. These precinct round figures are reconstructed from public CVR data, not certified native precinct round tables.

The 410,105 mayoral CVR records comprise 410,102 mapped records and 3 without precinct location. All are accounted for; the three unlocated records are never assigned to an arbitrary precinct. CVR record counts differ from the all-election ballot totals in the statement of vote. Election turnout is shown only where its published registration and ballot row is available. Thirteen precincts have withheld statement-of-vote rows; their public CVR-derived count is labelled separately and turnout stays unavailable.

Candidate shades use the lead over the next candidate divided by votes still counting in that precinct and round: up to 2, 2–5, 5–10, 10–20 and above 20 percentage points. Candidate colours stay consistent across rounds. Ties and no-continuing-vote areas are grey. Dots count precincts, not voters or population.
