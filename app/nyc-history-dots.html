How these estimates and groups work

The map is fixed to 2021 reporting areas. The 2021 votes are reported totals. The 2017 and 2025 votes are estimated by distributing source votes in proportion to overlapping map area within the same borough. This assumes votes are evenly spread within each source area, which can be inaccurate around parks, industrial land and uneven settlement.

Source areas with known missing component geometry, less than 99% map coverage, or excessive overlapping target geometry are withheld. Areas need usable two-side votes in all three elections, at least 99% mapped coverage from usable sources for each estimated year, and complete 2021 reporting geometry to enter groups. Withheld votes are accounted for in the data notes; they are not assigned to a nearby area. Grey areas remain selectable.

Voting similarity groups areas by their three-election patterns using the same Ward method as the county view. Political bands use fixed thresholds on the equally weighted three-election average. Other candidates and write-ins are excluded from two-side shares. In 2025 the opposing side combines Cuomo + Sliwa for this comparison only; Cuomo’s ballot line is not classified as Republican.

“Sensitive to allocation” means the estimated Democratic-side share changes by over 5 points under more-even or more-concentrated overlap weighting. This is an assumption check, not a confidence interval. An unflagged estimate is still an estimate.

Colours describe the group average: blue for the Democratic side, red for the opposing side, grey near an even split. Groups are descriptive and do not predict individual voters. Each dot is an area, not a voter or a resident.
