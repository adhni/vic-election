Sources, boundaries and colours

Both 2023 rounds use the City of Chicago’s 2023–2024 precinct map: 1,291 precincts in 50 wards. Ward results sum their precinct counts. Precinct returns are preserved by DataMade; each round’s candidate city totals, ward ballot totals and precinct counts reconcile to the official Chicago Board of Election Commissioners proclamation. The old official precinct export endpoint was inaccessible during import, so archived precinct cells have not been independently checked against an official precinct export.

The February 28 first round includes nine ballot candidates and six named write-ins. No candidate won outright; Johnson and Vallas advanced to the April 4 runoff. Colours identify candidates, not political parties. Each dot shows the local leader; it does not represent a separately elected mayor.

Darker shades mean a larger lead over the next candidate, divided by all recorded mayoral votes: up to 2, 2–5, 5–10, 10–20 and above 20 percentage points. Ties and areas with no mayoral votes are grey. Ballots without a recorded mayoral vote are preserved separately; the source does not distinguish blanks from overvotes in this import. Turnout uses each round’s own registered-voter count.
