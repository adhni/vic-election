What’s included?

Population mode uses 2020–2024 ACS five-year resident population estimates. It includes children and people who did not vote. The full view always has 3,103 equally sized dots. Each represents about the same number of residents; groups receive dots in proportion to their total population, with whole-dot rounding. Clicking a population dot shows a county contributing to that group. Some small counties receive no dot at this scale; all remain available on the map and in the county picker. Filters keep the same people-per-dot scale and show only matching dots. Political group assignments and county voting averages do not change.

3,103 comparable county reporting areas across 48 states, using the presidential elections of 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024. Connecticut is excluded because its reporting areas changed. Alaska only has statewide totals in these files. Washington, DC is also excluded. Some source areas combine neighbouring places.

Voting-similarity groups compare the Democratic percentage of votes for Democrats and Republicans across all five elections. Each county has equal weight. Filtering changes the counties shown, not their nationwide group assignments. Group descriptions and colours use the group average, not every county’s own result. These are past results, not predictions.
