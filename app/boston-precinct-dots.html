Sources, boundaries and colours

These are the November general elections, not the preliminary elections. Candidate votes come from the City of Boston’s official ward and precinct PDFs. Each precinct is one dot regardless of population or turnout. Write-ins and blank ballots remain in the details. Grouped other/write-in votes are not treated as one named candidate when choosing a local leader.

Colour strength describes the lead over the next named candidate, divided by all recorded candidate votes. Bands are up to 2, 2–5, 5–10, 10–20 and above 20 percentage points. Ties and zero-vote precincts are grey. Blank ballots are excluded from candidate vote shares.

The map is the City GIS October 2017 historical snapshot, including Ward 5 Precinct 2A. All three result tables join all 255 map identifiers. That establishes identifier coverage, not that 2013 or 2021 boundaries were exactly the same on election day. Those views explicitly use this historical reference. Current post-2022 boundaries are not substituted. No cross-year candidate alignment or vote allocation is inferred.
